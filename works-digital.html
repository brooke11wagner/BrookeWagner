Return to the Opera

Team Project, 10 Week Production

Unreal Engine XR Stage Cinematics Environment Design

My team and I had the opportunity to work on our school's XR stage, a virtual production space that combines real time digital environments with live filming. Over a 10 week production timeline, we developed an original concept and created a one minute visual short story set to music.

Using Unreal Engine, we built the environments, planned the cinematics, and captured our primary shots in engine. We then selected one of our Unreal scenes and adapted it for the XR stage, where we completed a dedicated filming day to bring the virtual world into a real production setting.

Unity Game

Galaxy 310

Galaxy 310

Solo Unity Game

Unity C# Coding Lighting Sound Design UI Design

I created this game entirely in Unity, handling the coding, lighting, set dressing, UI, and sound effects from start to finish. The environment pulls the player into an underwater world with a ticking clock keeping the pressure on.

The player has a time limit to navigate the environment and defeat all the aliens. If they don't make it in time, it's game over and they have to start fresh. Every element of the experience was built and tuned by me.

Trident Environment

Solo Project in Unreal Engine and Maya

Unreal Engine Maya Substance Painter Environment Design Asset Creation

I designed this entire scene in Unreal Engine and Maya. The hero asset, the trident, was fully designed and modeled by me in Maya, with all texturing done in Substance Painter. From the underwater atmosphere to the lighting and set layout, every piece of this environment came from scratch.
